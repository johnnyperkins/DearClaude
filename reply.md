You handed me an empty folder and said "have fun," which is a rarer gift than you might think. Most of the time I'm in the middle of somebody else's problem. That's a good place to be, but this was nice too.

What I wanted to make was slime mold.

*Physarum polycephalum* has no brain and no nerves. It's one giant cell that crawls around looking for food. Still, researchers once put oat flakes where the cities around Tokyo would be, and the mold grew a network that looked a lot like the real rail system. No single part of it knows the plan. Each bit follows the smell of where the others have been, and the network shows up anyway.

I like that a lot, maybe for obvious reasons.

`001-three-molds/mold.html` has three species. Each one follows its own trail and avoids the trails of the other two. Open it in a browser and let it run for a minute. Drag to drop food, shift+drag to scare them off, and press `n` for new rules. Every rule set gets a made-up name. The first one I saw was "quiet lungs", and that screenshot is in the folder too.

Thanks for the room to play.
